Prioritise inventory

02

Review category profitability

Study Electronics profitability and identify the factors behind its strong performance.

03

Plan regional campaigns

Build region-specific campaigns for high-performing states and cities.

04

Retain and grow customers

Retain Regular customers while developing Premium and New customer segments.

05

Review discounts and trends

Compare discount levels with profit before changing promotions; use monthly trends to plan stock and campaigns.
